Explore one policy's life

Premiums buy fund units; each month an admin fee, a management fee and a risk charge sell a few back. Fund value is the units times the price on that day. Pick another policy to see a different story.

Where a year of premiums goes

The annual statement (task t4): premiums paid in the year, minus the four charges. Pick a policy and a year.

Due dates and collection dates

Every premium due in the year after the extract date (task t1). A direct debit due on a weekend or a public holiday of canton Ticino is collected on the next business day; bank transfers keep their date. Orange outline: holiday. Hover a day for its numbers.
